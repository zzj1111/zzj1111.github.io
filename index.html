Zijian Zhang

PhD student in Electrical and Computer Engineering, University of Minnesota

University of Minnesota

Minneapolis, MN, USA

I am a PhD student in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Minnesota, advised by Prof. Mingyi Hong. I joined in Fall 2025 after receiving my B.S. in Computer Science from East China University of Science and Technology. Before that, I was a research assistant in Prof. Huaxiu Yao’s lab at UNC Chapel Hill, the Machine Learning Group at Microsoft Research Asia, and the InternLM2 team at Shanghai AI Lab.

My research focuses on self-improving LLM agents via reinforcement learning:

  • Agentic RL and multi-agent systems for real-world coding tasks such as GPU kernel optimization.
  • Recursive self-improvement of the RL post-training loop itself, where the system refines its own scaffolds, rewards, data, and evaluation to keep pushing the capability frontier.
