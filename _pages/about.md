I am a PhD student in the [Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering](https://cse.umn.edu/ece) at the [University of Minnesota](https://twin-cities.umn.edu/), advised by [Prof. Mingyi Hong](https://people.ece.umn.edu/~mhong/mingyi.html). I joined in Fall 2025 after receiving my B.S. in Computer Science from [East China University of Science and Technology](https://www.ecust.edu.cn/). Before that, I was a research assistant in [Prof. Huaxiu Yao's lab](https://www.huaxiuyao.io/) at UNC Chapel Hill, the [Machine Learning Group](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/group/machine-learning-research-group/) at Microsoft Research Asia, and the [InternLM2 team](https://internlm.intern-ai.org.cn/) at Shanghai AI Lab.

My research focuses on **self-improving LLM agents via reinforcement learning**:

- **Agentic RL and multi-agent systems** for real-world coding tasks such as GPU kernel optimization.
- **Recursive self-improvement** of the RL post-training loop itself, where the system refines its own scaffolds, rewards, data, and evaluation to keep pushing the capability frontier.
